## The channel

The show is *Broken News*, on the channel **BSN** (officially the Breaking
Story Network; nobody agrees what it stands for, design §3.4). The game
writes "BSN" and the show name wherever they appear, so the art itself stays
free of text unless a brief says otherwise (the logo, brief 05).

## The look

Same pixel-art house style as the rest of Career Crash (thick dark outline
#1b1f2a, flat colours with one shade tone, light from the top left, friendly
cartoon proportions). The studio is a slightly too-expensive American cable
news set: navy and red, chrome trim, a curved glossy desk, a video wall, fake
city skyline. The joke is that it looks serious and the people in it aren't.

## The two anchors (read before every brief)

**Brock Stetson Jr.** (American, he/him, 40s): Senior Anchor, self-appointed.
Big square jaw, very white teeth, a helmet of glossy chestnut hair that never
moves, a perma-tan, a red power tie, a navy suit one size too tight across the
shoulders, a small American-flag lapel pin. Loud, confident, wrong. Looks like
he's on a billboard for a car dealership.

**Philippa Featherstonehaugh** (British, she/her, 40s; pronounced "Fanshaw",
which Brock gets wrong every week): Co-Anchor. Sharp dark bob, reading glasses
she peers over, a tailored royal-blue blazer, pearl earrings, a fountain pen
always in hand. Dry, precise, quietly furious. Looks like she has a BBC
pension and a grudge.

They must read at a glance from each other: **red tie and big hair** against
**blue blazer and sharp bob**. Respectful caricature of the anchor *type*, not
of any real presenter: no likeness of real people, no real channel logos.
