## Importing

Upload the files (any names; say which is which if unsure). Claude:

- converts desk and field pictures to WebP in `apps/client/src/news/art/`
  (they replace the stand-ins straight away; Hamish's stand-in scaling stops
  once his art is in, because the art draws him short);
- slices the body sheets and imports the heads
  (`pnpm --filter @cc/art-pipeline puppets npc-news-<who>`, then `faces`),
  checking the previews, and gives Hamish and Bev their `persona`;
- converts the drops to `apps/client/src/news/art/drop-<x>.webp` (the glyphs
  go away);
- converts the plates and moves *Toaster Festival* to
  `news:wellington-toasters` and *The Launch* to `news:launch-party`.
