# Regards, Wall Street 1: body sheets (13 characters)

> Part of the Regards, Wall Street art set (`art/wallstreet/README.md`).
> Design: `docs/career-crash/13-regards-wall-street.md` §7.

Eleven office archetypes, one per stock-market sector, plus the floor's
referee and the Temp (any company without its own sector character). They
are workplace caricatures: the person every office has, at their most
themselves. Proud, overworked, a bit ridiculous, never mean-spirited.

**Reference to attach to every prompt:** `art/heads/_reference/body-sheet-example.webp`.

## Layout (copy `body-sheet-example.webp` exactly)

- **Canvas:** 1254 × 1254 px (or 1536 × 1024 landscape), transparent background.
- **Left half:** the full character standing, 3/4 view facing right, arms
  relaxed slightly away from the body, feet apart, **hands empty**.
- **Right half:** the same character cut into **13 separate pieces**, each with
  a clear transparent gap around it: head (with any hat, hair or glasses),
  torso, pelvis, left and right upper arm, left and right forearm with hand,
  left and right thigh, left and right shin, left and right shoe.
- Each piece is complete, outlined, with rounded joint ends.
- **Leave out:** held items (anything they carry goes on the body: a lanyard,
  a belt, a pocket, a holster), text, numbers, logos, floor shadows, background.

## Style preamble (paste first)

> Pixel-art character sheet for "Career Crash", a comedic 2D workplace
> brawler, matching the attached example sheet exactly in style and layout:
> chunky clean pixel art, thick dark outline (#1b1f2a), flat colours with one
> shade tone, light from the top left, big head (head to body about 1 : 1.4),
> friendly cartoon proportions. Transparent background. LEFT: the full
> character standing in 3/4 view facing right, hands empty. RIGHT: the same
> character cut into 13 separate paper-doll pieces laid out like the example
> (head, torso, pelvis, two upper arms, two forearms with hands, two thighs,
> two shins, two shoes), each piece outlined, with rounded joint ends and a
> clear gap around it. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no symbols
> on clothing, no shadow, no extra pieces.

## The characters

Main colour in brackets: use it for the main garment, so each sector reads at
a glance in a ten-way brawl. File: `art/sheets/npc-ws-<id>.png`.

**1. Information Technology: Scrum Master** (`ws-scrum-master`, blue #3b82f6)
> A relentlessly upbeat tech-company scrum master in his late twenties: a blue
> zip hoodie over a grey T-shirt, a lanyard with a keycard, a few colourful
> sticky notes stuck to the hoodie, slim joggers, chunky white trainers, a
> smartwatch, messy undercut hair, wireless earbuds. Lean, bouncing on his toes.

**2. Communication Services: Engagement Farmer** (`ws-engagement-farmer`, purple #a855f7)
> A social-media content producer in her twenties who films everything: a
> purple cropped puffer jacket, a small ring light strapped to a backpack
> harness behind her head, a phone holster on the hip, wide cargo trousers,
> platform trainers, a bright bucket hat, big hoop earrings. Pose: chin up,
> as if the camera is always on.

**3. Consumer Discretionary: Brand Ambassador** (`ws-brand-ambassador`, orange #f97316)
> A retail brand ambassador, early thirties, who loves a launch: an orange
> bomber jacket, crisp limited-edition trainers (plain, no marks), several
> paper shopping bags hanging from both forearms (part of the forearm
> pieces), a cap worn backwards, a big smile, a tidy beard. Athletic build.

**4. Consumer Staples: Shelf Stacker** (`ws-shelf-stacker`, green #22c55e)
> A cheerful supermarket night-shift stacker in her fifties: a green apron
> over a cardigan, a price gun holstered on the apron belt, a name badge with
> no writing, sensible black shoes, reading glasses on a chain, grey hair in a
> practical bun. Sturdy, unflappable, has seen everything.

**5. Health Care: Sales Rep** (`ws-sales-rep`, teal #14b8a6)
> A pharmaceutical sales rep in his forties: a white lab coat thrown over a
> sharp teal suit (he is not a doctor), a sample bag on a shoulder strap, a
> stethoscope worn purely as an accessory, polished shoes, a very white
> smile, perfect side-parted hair. Pose: leaning in, about to pitch.

**6. Financials: Vest Bro** (`ws-vest-bro`, indigo #6366f1)
> A young investment banker: a navy fleece vest over a pale blue button-down
> shirt with rolled sleeves, two phones in the vest pockets, slim chinos,
> loafers, an expensive-looking watch, swept-back hair, permanently tired
> eyes. Pose: weight on one leg, shoulders back.

**7. Energy: Roughneck** (`ws-roughneck`, burnt orange #b45309)
> An oil-rig roughneck in her thirties: burnt-orange coveralls with oil
> smudges, a white hard hat, safety goggles pushed up on the hat, heavy work
> gloves, steel-toe boots, a tool belt with a big wrench, a braid down her
> back. Broad shoulders, planted stance.

**8. Industrials: Forklift Certified** (`ws-forklift-certified`, yellow #eab308)
> A warehouse forklift operator in his fifties who takes safety very
> seriously: a yellow hi-vis vest over a flannel shirt, ear defenders round
> the neck, a clipboard clipped to his belt, work trousers with knee pads,
> steel-toe boots, a grey moustache. Heavy-set, arms ready to direct traffic.

**9. Materials: Quarry Boss** (`ws-quarry-boss`, stone grey #a8a29e)
> A quarry foreman in her forties covered in stone dust: dusty grey work
> jacket, a white hard hat, a pickaxe-shaped holster on her back (part of the
> torso piece), heavy gloves tucked in a pocket, cargo work trousers, worn
> boots, short cropped hair. Strong, dusty, cheerful.

**10. Utilities: Meter Reader** (`ws-meter-reader`, lime #84cc16)
> A utility-company meter reader in his sixties, steady as a power line: a
> lime hi-vis jacket over a knitted jumper, a torch clipped to the jacket, a
> handheld meter on a lanyard, flat cap, comfortable trousers, walking boots,
> neat white beard. Slow, patient, unbothered.

**11. Real Estate: Open House Agent** (`ws-open-house-agent`, pink #ec4899)
> A real-estate agent in her thirties ready for a viewing: a pink blazer over
> a white top, a small key bunch on the belt, a SOLD-style sign shape slung on
> the back (blank, no letters), slim trousers, heeled boots, sunglasses
> pushed up in glossy hair, a bright professional smile.

**12. The Floor Specialist (referee)** (`ws-specialist`, red #dc2626 with navy)
> An old-school stock-exchange floor trader in his sixties who referees the
> brawl: a red trading jacket with a blank number badge, a navy tie, a
> whistle on a cord, a pencil behind the ear, a fistful of order slips tucked
> in the top pocket, comfortable shoes, white hair, a booming voice in his
> posture. Arms raised as if shouting hand signals.

**13. The Temp** (`ws-temp`, grey #94a3b8)
> A lost first-day office temp in his early twenties: an ill-fitting grey
> suit one size too big, a visitor lanyard, a coffee-stained shirt, a tie
> tied wrong, scuffed trainers instead of shoes, floppy hair. Shoulders up,
> slightly worried.

## Importing

```
pnpm --filter @cc/art-pipeline puppets npc-ws-scrum-master npc-ws-engagement-farmer npc-ws-brand-ambassador npc-ws-shelf-stacker npc-ws-sales-rep npc-ws-vest-bro npc-ws-roughneck npc-ws-forklift-certified npc-ws-quarry-boss npc-ws-meter-reader npc-ws-open-house-agent npc-ws-specialist npc-ws-temp
```

Files named `npc-<persona>` import as the persona `npc.<persona>`. Every
company in a sector wears its archetype (`persona` in `sectors`,
`markets/stocks.json`); the referee and the Temp are personas of their own.
Check the labelled previews in `tools/art-pipeline/out/puppets/`, then
re-run `pnpm balance --markets-flat --market=stocks` (new bodies, same moves:
it should still be within ±25%).

attach it to their head prompts.
