# Crypto Bros 10: page art

> Part of the Crypto Bros art set (`art/cryptobro/README.md`).

Art for the page around the fight, not the fight itself. The page title and
text are added by the page, so the art has no words.

**References to attach:** `art/arenas/office.png` (look), and any finished
bro body sheets from `art/sheets/npc-bro-*.png`.

## A. Logo mark

- **File:** `art/ui/cryptobro-logo.png`, **512 × 512**, transparent.

> Pixel-art emblem for "Career Crash", chunky clean pixel art, thick dark
> outline (#1b1f2a), flat colours with one shade tone. A cartoon fist in a
> boxing glove punching upward through a green candlestick chart bar, with a
> small rocket flying past and a few plain gold coins (no symbols) bursting
> out. Fits in a circle. Transparent background, no text, no letters, no logos.

## B. Share image

- **File:** `art/ui/cryptobro-share.png`, **1200 × 630**, opaque (link previews).
- Leave the **left 45%** calm (a dark gradient area): the page writes the
  title there.

> [Same style as A, opaque] A wide pixel-art scene, 1200 × 630: a chaotic
> brawl on a crypto trading floor at night, seen in 3/4 view, on the right
> side of the picture. Five cartoon crypto bros mid-fight (laser-eye
> sunglasses, a shiba-ears headband, a ski jacket, a violet running vest,
> a pastel blazer), a shiba dog biting an ankle, plain gold coins flying, a
> wall of red and green charts behind them, a brass bell. The left 45% of
> the picture is a calm dark purple-to-teal gradient with a faint chart line
> and nothing else. No text, no numbers, no logos, no real people.

## C. Ticker portrait frame

- **File:** `art/ui/ticker-frame.png`, **256 × 256**, transparent (a frame
  the page draws a bro's head inside, in the ticker strip).

> [Same style as A] A square pixel-art portrait frame with rounded corners,
> like a trading card: a dark navy border with a thin gold inner line, a
> small blank tab at the bottom (for the ticker label), a tiny up-arrow
> notch top-right and a tiny down-arrow notch bottom-right (plain shapes).
> The centre is fully transparent. No text.

## Importing

These go straight into the page build (no atlas step). Claude converts
them to WebP on import.
