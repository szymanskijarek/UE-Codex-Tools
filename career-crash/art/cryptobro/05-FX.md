# Crypto Bros 5: effects

> **Status:** delivered (5 October 2026). The sheets came in other sizes, so they were repacked into equal cells; the liquidation uses the stamp, margin call, ceiling launch and coin shower. Laser eyes, gas fog and FUD cloud wait for the moves and scene events that use them.

> Part of the Crypto Bros art set (`art/cryptobro/README.md`). Same rules as
> the main game's `art/FX_BRIEF.md`.

The signature moments of the Crypto Bros floor: The OG's laser eyes, and the
big dramatic **liquidation** when a crashing coin is knocked out (a margin-call
klaxon, red strobe, a giant LIQUIDATED stamp, the bro launched through the
ceiling, coins raining down).

## House style (paste before every prompt)

> Pixel-art game effect for "Career Crash", a comedic 2D workplace brawler.
> Chunky, clean pixel art with a thick dark outline (#1b1f2a) on solid shapes,
> flat colour fills with one shade tone, bright readable palette, cartoon
> comic-book energy. Transparent background, no ground shadow, no text, no
> letters, no numbers, no labels, no logos, no coin symbols, no watermark, no
> frame, no characters, no blood or gore.

**References to attach:** `art/items/fx-hit.png` and `art/items/fx-ko-stars.png`.

## Rules (the importer relies on them)

1. **One sheet per effect,** a single row of frames, left to right in playing order.
2. **Equal cells:** the canvas is exactly `frames × cell width` wide and one cell
   tall. Nothing crosses a cell edge; at least 6% empty margin per frame.
3. **Same origin in every frame:** centred, or on a ground line 85% down for
   floor effects.
4. **The animation lives in the frames:** small first frame, peak, then break
   up (the game doesn't fade or scale frames).
5. **Directional** effects travel to the right (the game mirrors them).
6. At most ~12 flat colours per sheet; no soft gradients.

### Prompts

**1. `laser-eyes`: `art/items/fx-laser-eyes.png`, 2048 × 128, 4 frames, travels right**
> A cartoon laser-beam animation in 4 frames: two parallel bright red beams
> with white-hot cores and a thick dark outline, starting from the left edge
> and shooting right. (1) two short red sparks at the left, (2) the beams
> half way across, (3) beams at full length to the right edge with a small
> starburst where they end, (4) beams thinning into broken red dashes.

**2. `liquidated-stamp`: `art/items/fx-liquidated-stamp.png`, 2560 × 256, 5 frames**
> A giant red rubber-stamp impression slamming down, in 5 frames: a wide
> rectangular red ink stamp with a thick double border and a rough inky
> edge, the inside left blank (the game writes the word). (1) the stamp
> shape small and high, a motion blur above it, (2) huge and slightly
> tilted as it hits, with ink splatter flecks (red ink, not blood), (3)
> settled, slightly smaller, a few flecks, (4) the same with a faint
> shockwave ring, (5) the same, ink a little faded. The middle of the
> stamp is empty.

**3. `margin-call`: `art/items/fx-margin-call.png`, 1024 × 256, 4 frames**
> A cartoon alarm animation in 4 frames: a red rotating emergency beacon
> light with red light rays and a jagged red warning burst behind it. (1)
> beacon with rays pointing left, (2) rays pointing up with a big burst,
> (3) rays pointing right, (4) rays pointing down with a smaller burst.
> Loops.

**4. `coin-shower`: `art/items/fx-coin-shower.png`, 1536 × 256, 6 frames**
> A burst of shiny cartoon gold coins (plain round coins, no symbols, a
> simple rim and a white glint), shot up from a point on a ground line 85%
> down the cell, fanning out, then raining down. (1) a flash with three
> coins leaving, (2) eight coins rising in a fan, spinning (some edge-on),
> (3) coins at the top of the arc, (4) coins falling, (5) coins bouncing on
> the ground line, (6) four coins lying flat.

**5. `ceiling-launch`: `art/items/fx-ceiling-launch.png`, 1280 × 256, 5 frames**
> A cartoon "burst through the ceiling" animation in 5 frames, seen from
> below: square white ceiling tiles cracking open in a star-shaped hole.
> (1) a crack in one tile, (2) tiles bursting upward and outward with
> plaster dust, (3) a ragged dark hole with tile pieces flying, (4) dust
> puffs and a few falling tile pieces, (5) the ragged hole with drifting
> dust.

**6. `gas-fog`: `art/items/fx-gas-fog.png`, 2048 × 256, 4 frames of 512 × 256, loops**
> A low, wide cloud of bright green cartoon fog with darker green swirls,
> lying on a ground line 85% down the cell, in 4 looping frames: the swirls
> turn and the edges ripple, one or two bubbles rise. Semi-transparent edges.

**7. `fud-cloud`: `art/items/fx-fud-cloud.png`, 1024 × 256, 4 frames, loops**
> A small grumpy grey storm cloud with a dark underside, raining a few drops
> and flickering one tiny yellow lightning zigzag, in 4 looping frames: the
> cloud puffs bigger and smaller, the lightning flickers on in frame 3.
