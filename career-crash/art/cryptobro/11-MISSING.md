## Rules for every image

- **Exact canvas sizes.** The importers cut images by size. Each part gives
  the canvas; please deliver exactly that (earlier batches came at other
  sizes and had to be rescaled or repacked).
- **Transparent background** everywhere (no white, no checkerboard).
- **No text, letters, numbers, logos or coin symbols** (no ₿, Ξ, Ð) anywhere.
- **No real people**, no look-alikes. **No gore.**
- **House look:** chunky clean pixel art, thick dark outline (#1b1f2a), flat
  colours with one shade tone, light from the top left.
