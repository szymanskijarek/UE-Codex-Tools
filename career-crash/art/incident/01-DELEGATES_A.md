# Diplomatic Incident 01: delegate bodies, batch A

> **Status:** not delivered yet.

> Part of the Diplomatic Incident art set (`art/incident/README.md`, read its
> *How we portray countries* first). Design: `docs/career-crash/09-diplomatic-incident.md`.

Ten delegates: England, Scotland, Wales, Ireland, France, Germany, Italy, Spain, Portugal, Netherlands. Each is their country's champion at a
summit that has turned into a brawl: proud, likeable, dressed in a
loving version of their national dress, with a summit **delegate lanyard**
(a blank white pass on a ribbon) on top.

**Reference to attach to every prompt:** `art/heads/_reference/body-sheet-example.webp`
(and `art/sheets/npc-bro-btc.png` for the level of detail).

## Layout (copy `body-sheet-example.webp` exactly)

- **Canvas:** 1254 × 1254 px (or 1536 × 1024 landscape), transparent background.
- **Left half:** the full character standing, 3/4 view facing right, arms
  relaxed slightly away from the body, feet apart, **hands empty**.
- **Right half:** the same character cut into **13 separate pieces**, each with
  a clear transparent gap around it: head (with hat, hair, headband), torso,
  pelvis, left and right upper arm, left and right forearm with hand, left and
  right thigh, left and right shin, left and right shoe.
- **Held things go on the body:** sticks, bats, umbrellas, instruments, food
  are strapped to the back or hooked on the belt, never in the hands.
- A kilt, skirt, dress or robe goes on the **pelvis** piece and may hang over
  the thighs; long sleeves stay on the arm pieces.

> (no caricature). Transparent background. LEFT: the full character standing
> in 3/4 view facing right, hands empty. RIGHT: the same character cut into
> 13 separate paper-doll pieces laid out like the example (head, torso,
> pelvis, two upper arms, two forearms with hands, two thighs, two shins, two
> shoes), each piece outlined, with rounded joint ends and a clear gap around
> it. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no shadow, no extra pieces.
