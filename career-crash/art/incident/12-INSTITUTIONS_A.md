# Diplomatic Incident 12: the Institutions, batch A (UN, NATO, ICC, Big Tech)

> **Status:** not delivered yet. Design exploration: these gatecrashers aren't
> built yet (`docs/career-crash/09-diplomatic-incident.md` §7.2); the art can
> come first.

> Part of the Diplomatic Incident art set (`art/incident/README.md`).

The summit's gatecrashers are **the institutions**: international bodies and
global industries who swoop into the brawl, do very little and leave. The
satire is on their reputation (talking instead of acting, red tape, PR),
never on a country or a people.

**Rules for these pictures:**
- **No emblems, logos or flags** of any real organisation: no UN wreath, no
  NATO star, no company marks. Colour and costume tell them apart.
- **No real people:** no likeness of any secretary-general, judge, official
  or tech founder. Every character is made up.
- **No weapons:** peacekeepers carry clipboards, gendarmes carry whistles
  and handcuffs.

Nine pictures: eight body sheets and one horse. **Heads come after** (table
at the end; send them in two halves of 16).

**Reference to attach:** `art/heads/_reference/body-sheet-example.webp` for
the bodies (and `art/sheets/npc-bro-btc.png` for detail).

## Layout and style

Same as every body sheet: 1254 × 1254 px, transparent. LEFT: the full
character in 3/4 view facing right, hands empty. RIGHT: the same character in
13 separate outlined pieces (head, torso, pelvis, two upper arms, two
forearms with hands, two thighs, two shins, two shoes). Paste the style
preamble from `01-DELEGATES_A.md` first.

## The bodies (`art/sheets/npc-inst-<id>.png`)

**1. UN Special Envoy** (`inst-un-envoy`, sky blue #5b92e5 with white)
> A weary, earnest special envoy in her fifties: a sky-blue skirt suit, a
> white blouse, a sky-blue scarf, a thick ring binder of resolutions under
> the arm (strapped to the body), sensible shoes, a lanyard with a blank pass,
> greying hair in a bun, reading glasses, an expression of deep concern.

**2. UN Peacekeeper** (`inst-un-peacekeeper`, sky blue helmet, khaki)
> A gentle, slightly bewildered peacekeeper in his twenties: a shiny
> sky-blue helmet (no markings), a sky-blue scarf, a khaki shirt and
> trousers, a clipboard and a stack of leaflets clipped to the belt (no
> weapon of any kind), sturdy boots, a hopeful face.

**3. The Alliance Official** (`inst-nato-official`, navy #003c78 with white)
> An immaculate, faintly smug official in his fifties: a perfectly pressed
> navy suit, a white shirt, a navy tie with a small star pattern (no emblem),
> a white pocket square, polished riding boots with spurs (he rides
> everywhere), a speech in the breast pocket, silver hair swept back, chin
> held high. Drawn standing; the game puts him on the horse.

**4. Alliance Aide** (`inst-nato-aide`, navy and grey)
> A harried aide in her twenties: a navy blazer, grey trousers, a headset, a
> tablet in a holster on the belt, a stack of press releases under the arm,
> running shoes (she does all the walking), a tight ponytail.

**5. ICC Magistrate** (`inst-icc-magistrate`, black robe with white)
> An elderly, stern magistrate: a long black judicial robe with a white
> lace jabot, a small black cap, a rolled-up arrest warrant tucked into the
> belt, a gavel hanging from a cord, half-moon glasses, a white moustache,
> a deeply disapproving frown.

**6. Gendarme** (`inst-icc-gendarme`, navy-black with red and white)
> A comedic post-war (1940s–50s) Belgian/French-style gendarme: a navy
> kepi with a red top and a short peak, a short dark navy cape over a tunic
> with brass buttons, white gloves, a white belt with a pair of handcuffs and
> a whistle on a chain (no gun, no baton), dark trousers with a red side
> stripe, polished black boots, a big curled moustache. Proud, pompous, a bit
> round.

**7. Big Tech Founder** (`inst-tech-founder`, grey hoodie with teal #14b8a6)
> A generic tech founder in his thirties (no resemblance to any real
> person): a grey hoodie under a puffy teal gilet, slim black jeans, bright
> white sneakers, a smartwatch, wireless earbuds, a lanyard with a blank
> conference badge, a spare phone clipped to the belt, tousled hair, a
> wide, unblinking "we're changing the world" smile.

**8. Growth Hacker** (`inst-tech-hacker`, teal and black)
> An eager growth hacker in her twenties: a black T-shirt, a teal company
> fleece tied around the waist, cargo trousers, a backpack covered in blank
> sticker shapes, three phones on lanyards round the neck, a laptop under the
> arm strapped to her body, round glasses, a buzzcut, energy-drink energy.
