Broken News, on BSN

The week's news as a brawl, then a game. Two cable-news anchors, Brock Stetson Jr. and Philippa Featherstonehaugh, read a real story. The conversation spirals, they brawl across the desk, and then you play the story in a short free minigame. Every episode ends with what really happened and a link to the source.

On air now

  • Man vs Emu: an escaped emu called Side Neck wrestles a man on a Devon road, and wins. Play Wrestle the Bird: grab, block or duck on the beat to protect your trousers.
  • Chairs Recalled: forty thousand office chairs recalled for "spinning with intent". Play Spinning With Intent: steer a runaway chair through the TV station and into the anchor.

Part of Career Crash, the free browser brawler where ordinary jobs fight it out. Also on careercrash.org: Crypto Bros and Diplomatic Incident.
