# 11 — Broken News: the cast bible (v0.1, 9 October 2026)

Who the people of BSN are, so every script writes them the same way. Design
of the show itself: `10-broken-news.md`. Art: `career-crash/art/news/`.

## 1. The rule above all: everyone is likable

The show is a pile-up of people who are bad at one thing and quietly good at
another. Viewers should want every one of them back next week.

1. **Everyone is good at something,** and the script lets them be good at it
   at least once an episode, even mid-disaster.
2. **Nobody is cruel.** They bicker, they brawl, they get things wrong, but no
   one sneers, and no one is mocked for who they are (looks, accent, origin,
   body). The joke is the situation, the pomposity, the job.
3. **Their flaws are sincere.** Brock isn't pretending to be dim; Chase
   really believes it's a hurricane. Sincere is funny *and* warm.
4. **They make up.** The brawl is slapstick between people who like each
   other. The sign-off can land a small kind beat (a hand on a shoulder, a
   shared mug, an apology to Jeff).
5. **One mean line is the limit,** and it goes to whoever has the most
   status in the scene, never down.

## 3. The desk

### Brock Stetson Jr.: the rock star

*Dim, handsome, charming, and the happiest man on television.*

- **The joke:** he's wrong about everything, with total confidence. He
  mispronounces Philippa's name every week. He believes he owns the network
  (the initials match). He has a regional Emmy (Best Hair).
- **Why we love him:** he's generous. He gives every guest the biggest
  welcome of their life, believes every word anyone tells him, and is
  thrilled for other people's wins. He answers every viewer letter by hand.
  When Philippa is right, he's *proud* of her.
- **The rock star:** he plays air guitar on the stings, calls the desk "the
  stage", throws pens into the crowd (there is no crowd), and fronts a
  covers band nobody has heard (*Brock & the Breaking Stories*). Studio
  lights find him; he finds the camera.
- **Wants:** to be loved. **Fears:** dead air.
- **Voice:** short, loud, sure. Catchphrases he gets slightly wrong. Never
  sarcastic: Brock doesn't do irony.
- **With others:** Philippa is his work wife whether she likes it or not;
  Chase is his best bud; Jeff is "the best in the business" (he's just been
  hit by a lighting rig).

> *"Folks, I've read the report. Well, I've held it. Philippa read it."*
> *"Phil-ippa Feathers-tone-HOFF, everybody!"*
> *"Jeff! Great drop, buddy. Real height on that one."*

### Philippa Featherstonehaugh: the competent one who can't do people

*Deadpan, literal, socially baffled, and always right.*

- **The joke:** she has no small talk. Long pauses where warmth should go.
  She takes idioms literally ("Who let the cat out of which bag?"), asks
  questions that are oddly specific and slightly too honest ("Is a sandwich
  shop without sandwiches just a shop?"), and reads compliments as
  accusations. Her attempts at banter come out like a tax return.
- **Why we love her:** she's the best journalist in the building. She has
  the real story by the second line. She never lies, never panics, and she
  *is* trying: every episode she makes one attempt at warmth, and it's
  touching because it's so awkward.
- **Wants:** the facts, done properly. **Secret:** she envies Brock's ease
  with people, and would rather be hit by Jeff's lighting rig than say so.
- **Voice:** precise, flat, a beat too long. Uses the `awkward` pause (10
  §4.4) more than anyone. Says "Right." as a full sentence.
- **With others:** corrects Brock, but defends him to anyone else; Rupert is
  her old flame, which she handles by being even more formal; Dusty is the
  only one she enjoys talking to, because he talks in data.

> *"Thank you, Brock. That was almost a sentence."*
> *(awkward pause)* *"I've been told to say something warm. … Radiators."*
> *"For the record, it's Featherstonehaugh. Pronounced Fanshaw. Spelt
> incorrectly by the universe."*

### Jeff: the man who is never on screen

*The studio technician. You will never see him. You will see what he drops.*

- **The joke:** every few weeks something falls on Brock: a lighting rig, a
  boom mic, a sandbag, a tray of coffees, a ceiling tile, Jeff's own office
  chair (the rogue chair: it got away from him). A voice from off screen
  shouts *"SORRY, BROCK!"*
- **Why we love him:** Brock loves him. Brock never once blames Jeff, and
  Jeff never stops apologising. They're the show's most functional
  relationship.
- **Rules:** never shown, not even a hand. Speaks only from off screen, one
  short line at a time. Never drops anything on anyone but Brock (Brock
  insists: "Only me, Jeff. I can take it.").
- **Kevin** is Jeff's intern. When Jeff drops something, Kevin is the one
  who appears on screen to pick it up.

> *"(off) Sorry, Brock!"* · *"(off) That one was the union's fault."*
> Brock: *"You're doing great, Jeff!"*

## 4. The field

### Chase Hurley: Brock's best friend, live from the eye of the storm

*Exaggerates everything; loyal to the bone.*

- **The joke:** every story is the biggest story of his career. A light
  breeze is a hurricane, a queue is a riot, three pigeons are a "situation".
  He's always on the wrong side of a barrier, leaning into wind that isn't
  there.
- **The friendship:** Brock and Chase are *the boys*. They've been best
  friends since college and they have a plan for everything (the plans are
  terrible). Chase backs up Brock's wrong claims with live "evidence" from
  the field; Brock backs up Chase's exaggerations from the desk. They have a
  secret handshake they try to do through the screen. Brock is the cool one
  who always lands on his feet, mug in hand; Chase is the loyal one who
  follows him into every disaster and would do it again.
- **Why we love him:** he's brave about things that don't need courage,
  and he'd walk through a real storm for a friend.
- **Voice:** shouting over silence. Superlatives. "Stets!" for Brock.
- **With Philippa:** she fact-checks him live; he takes it as a compliment.

> *"Stets, I've been out here eleven minutes and I have SEEN THINGS."*
> *"Brock, buddy, they said it couldn't be done. They didn't say what."*

### Rupert Fennimore-Twistleton: gravitas, wasted

*A real foreign correspondent posted to sandwich shops.*

- **The joke:** reports trivia with the solemnity of a war zone; never sure
  which country he's in; signs off in a language he doesn't speak.
- **Why we love him:** he *has* been to real war zones, and he gives a
  closed bakery the same respect, because to the baker it matters. Kind to
  every local, always.
- **With Philippa:** old flame; both pretend not to notice; both notice.

> *"Philippa. I am standing outside a sandwich shop which, as of eleven
> o'clock this morning, has no sandwiches."*

### Hamish Tuck: live (ish) from Wellington

*The BSN Asia-Pacific Bureau. The bureau is Hamish.*

- **The joke:** the satellite link is so slow that Hamish always answers the
  *previous* question, so every exchange is one line out of sync (the script
  uses `pause: "delay"`, 10 §13.5). And he's already in tomorrow, so he keeps
  accidentally spoiling the next day's news. The BSN newsroom has labelled
  Wellington *the most boring town in the world* on the chyron; Hamish hotly
  disputes this, then reports a repainted bench with breathless excitement.
- **Why we love him:** he's delighted by everything, he loves his town and
  its people, and he's the only one who never complains about the delay. The
  wind (the town's real claim to fame) is constantly trying to take his
  notes, his hat and occasionally him (`weather: "wind"`).
- **Short:** Hamish is short, never as the joke itself but so the world
  around him looms: Brock: *"Man, those toasters are MASSIVE!"* Hamish:
  *"They're regular toasters, Brock."* Nobody mocks his height; the props do
  the work.
- **Rules:** affectionate. The joke is BSN's ignorance and Hamish's
  enthusiasm, never the town's people or New Zealand accents; no real
  landmarks or people in the art.
- **Voice:** sunny, unhurried, "Yeah, nah" and "sweet as" used sparingly.
  His clock on screen says **TOMORROW**.

> Philippa: *"Hamish, can you hear us?"* · Hamish: *"…Good evening Brock,
> and yes, it's going really well here."*
> *"Big news from Wellington tonight: the wind has stopped. Sorry, it's
> started again."*
> *"Can't say too much, but you're going to love Thursday."*

### Bev Fizzwilliam: live from the launch party

*Entertainment & Lifestyle Editor. Always at a launch. Always holding a glass.*

- **The joke:** she reports from red carpets, galas and the opening of
  anything, a little tipsy, with a risqué line in every report that the
  studio scrambles to cut off. She calls everyone "darling", including the
  camera.
- **Why we love her:** she's warm, glamorous and the only person on BSN who
  says what everyone is thinking. She gets the gossip right, she's kind to
  the waiters, and she adores Philippa ("my favourite little iceberg").
- **Rules:** innuendo only: double meanings, a raised eyebrow, a cut-off
  word, a `bleep`. Never explicit, never about a real person, never about
  anyone's body, never mean. Tipsy means bubbly, never sloppy or sad.
- **Voice:** long vowels, champagne pauses, "Darling…" She ends reports
  with a toast.

> *"The theme tonight is 'nothing to declare'. I declared everything,
> darling. Twice."*
> *"Lovely firm handshake on the new CEO. Very firm. I checked."*
> *"Back to you, Brock. Back to you. Front to you also, if you're free."*
> *(the studio cuts her off)*

## 5. The guests at the desk

### Brody Kale: Sports & Wellness

*Obsessed with healthy living; has never understood a single question.*

- **The joke:** everything he's told, he hears as a wellness question. The
  market crashed? "Crashes are just your body asking for electrolytes." A
  team lost 6–0? "Six is a lot of reps, Brock." He also doesn't follow
  sport: he only knows which players hydrate. Replaces Coach Biff (Biff's
  look and key are retired).
- **Why we love him:** he's the most encouraging man alive. He tells Brock
  his posture is improving. He brings everyone a smoothie. He thinks the
  brawl is a group workout and he's *so* proud of them.
- **Look (for the brief):** 30s, unreasonably fit, a sleeveless sports-desk
  blazer over a compression top, a sweatband, a giant water bottle, a smart
  watch he checks constantly, a protein shaker, a green smoothie.
- **Voice:** upbeat, earnest, counts reps out loud.

> *"Brock, I'm not a doctor, but I am a doctor of positivity."*
> *"Great question, Philippa. The answer is kale."*

### Dusty Gale: Weather

*A real meteorologist on a short-term contract who takes the weather
personally.*

- **The joke:** he names clouds, apologises for rain, takes a cold front as
  a betrayal, and is desperate for the slot to be longer than 40 seconds.
- **Why we love him:** he has a PhD and genuine joy. Philippa is the only one
  who listens to his isobars, and the two of them nerding out is the show's
  sweetest scene.
- **Look (redesigned, brief 11 A):** his own silhouette, so he no longer
  reads as another Brock or Wes: **tall, lanky, a little stooped**, big
  round glasses that fog up, freckles, a **too-big yellow rain mac over a
  teal cardigan with a cloud pattern**, a pocket barometer on a chain, and
  **weathervane hair**: a red-ginger cowlick that always points the way the
  wind is blowing (indoors too).

> *"That's not a cloud, Brock. That's Gerald. And Gerald is upset."*

### Kevin: The Intern

Has never been allowed to speak. Picks up whatever Jeff dropped. One day he
will speak, and it will be the season finale.

## 7. The relationship map

- **Brock ↔ Philippa:** work spouses. He's sunshine, she's a filing cabinet;
  each is the other's favourite person and would never say it.
- **Brock ↔ Chase:** *the boys*. Plans, handshakes, mutual backup, no brakes.
- **Brock ↔ Jeff:** the falling object and the man who forgives it.
- **Philippa ↔ Rupert:** old flame, extreme politeness.
- **Philippa ↔ Dusty:** the data friendship.
- **Philippa ↔ Bev:** Bev adores her; Philippa doesn't know what to do with
  her hands.
- **Hamish ↔ everyone:** one line behind, always smiling.
- **Brody ↔ everyone:** a smoothie for each of you.

## 8. Which character drives which story

| Story type | Lead | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Something fell over / nothing happened | Chase | he'll make it the storm of the century |
| Pomp, ceremony, small crisis abroad | Rupert | gravitas, wasted |
| Anything far away, or an "update" | Hamish | the delay and tomorrow do the jokes |
| Celebrities, launches, awards, money's glamour | Bev | the party and the innuendo |
| Sport, health fads, diets, records | Brody | misunderstands it as wellness |
| Weather, climate oddities | Dusty | he takes it personally |
| Brawl starters with no argument | Jeff | something drops on Brock |
